# Usage guide

1. **Add photos** – drag files onto the canvas (drop on a slot to target it), click an empty slot, or use *Photos → Add photos*. Empty slots fill automatically.
2. **Pick a layout** – *Layout* tab. N×M means N rows × M columns (2×1 = two stacked panels).
3. **Adjust a photo** – click it. Drag to reposition, scroll to zoom, or use the right-hand *Properties* panel (zoom, position, rotate, flip, Fit / Fill / Reset, enhancement sliders and preset filters).
4. **Style** – *Style* tab for background and palette; *Layout* tab for gaps, corners and frames.
5. **Add content** – *Add* tab: text, timestamp, username watermark, logo, emoji. Drag to move, scroll or use the corner handle to resize. Objects snap to the canvas centre.
6. **Organise** – *Layers* tab: hide, lock, reorder, duplicate, delete.
7. **Templates** – *Templates* tab: apply a ready-made look (your photos stay), save your own, or duplicate the design with photos cleared.
8. **Check safe areas** – tick *Safe-area overlay* under the canvas; keep text out of the red bands.
9. **Export** – right panel → *Export*: format, quality, resolution, file name, folder, then **Export** (Ctrl+E). *Batch export* turns the whole photo library into numbered Stories.
10. **Save work** – *Save* (Ctrl+S) writes an editable `.cmjstory.json`; *Open* restores it. Unsaved work is auto-saved locally and offered back after a crash.

## Shortcuts
Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo/redo · Ctrl+S save · Ctrl+O open · Ctrl+E export · Ctrl+D duplicate · Delete remove · Arrows nudge (Shift ×10) · + / − zoom photo · Esc deselect.
